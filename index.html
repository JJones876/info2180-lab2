The Challenges of Mining the Skills and Crafting the Degree

October 2, 2026 by Jonathon Jones

Having a heavy workload can put pressure on both the body and mind. As a college student, you have to balance completing assignments, studying, and finding some free time for yourself. Final exams can also be very challenging, and one of the toughest parts is having to perform well in both coursework and examinations to successfully complete a computing course.

Some ways that I deal with these challenges are taking consistent breaks, collaborating with my peers, and participating in physical activities. I also try to motivate and reward myself for staying disciplined and developing the habit of working hard. I know that the skills I gain from my degree can help me build a successful career in the future.
